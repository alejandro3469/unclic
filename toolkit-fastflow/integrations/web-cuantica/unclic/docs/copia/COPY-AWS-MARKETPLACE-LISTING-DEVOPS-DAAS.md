## Pestaña / Sección: Overview

### Título

**DevOps as a Service — Pipeline and deployment pack**

### Cuerpo principal

Implementation of CI/CD and pipeline-as-code practices is crucial for the success of development teams, regardless of size. By reducing manual workflow bottlenecks in both development and production, it enables repeatable delivery. Our team takes care of designing and operating the pipeline (Jenkins, Gitea, optional registry, deploy), allowing your team to focus on product development and achieve predictable results with a clear scope and verified delivery.

### Challenges faced

Startups and SMEs often face challenges when adopting CI/CD:

- **Budget and capacity** — Limited budget, manpower, or infrastructure to implement and maintain pipelines and tooling.
- **In-house expertise** — Lack of dedicated teams proficient in pipeline design, Jenkins, and deployment automation.
- **Scope and expectations** — Unclear scope leads to misalignment; we address this with written scope and a defined number of revision rounds.
- **Integration complexity** — Integrating repos, build tools (e.g. Maven), and deployment targets (EC2, Kubernetes) into a cohesive pipeline can be complex, especially with existing or legacy systems.
- **Continuous improvement** — DevOps and pipeline evolution require ongoing refinement; we offer subscription or per-sprint engagement so you can iterate without hiring full-time.

### Scaling with our support

Our team provides the tools and support needed to implement pipeline-as-code and deployment automation—without you having to carry full-time DevOps expertise in-house. We deliver production-grade pipeline design and operation (Jenkinsfile, Jenkins, Gitea, optional registry, Terraform when applicable), with clear scope, documentation in repo, and delivery verified in the agreed environment. Support is provided in business hours according to the agreement (we do not offer 24/7 support).

### Key deliverables

- **Infrastructure as Code (IaC) when applicable** — Terraform for networks, instances, and security; documentation and repeatability in repo.
- **Pipeline lifecycle** — Automated build, test, package, and deploy; visibility and logs in Jenkins; optional registry for versioned images and rollback.
- **Pipeline as Code** — Jenkinsfile versioned in Git; multibranch, declarative; changes reviewable and repeatable.
- **Support for standard CI/CD tooling** — Jenkins, Gitea, Maven, Docker; optional Kubernetes and Terraform depending on scope.
- **Configuration and code in repo** — Pipeline definition and documentation live in your repo or ours by agreement; no black box.
- **Transition support** — Guidance for moving from manual or ad-hoc deployment to a defined pipeline (dev, staging, production as agreed).
- **Clear scope and documentation** — Written proposal, defined revision rounds, and delivery criteria; no “unlimited revisions” without agreement.
- **Templates and reuse** — Use of proven Jenkinsfile and IaC patterns; environments can be adapted or extended by scope.

### Service highlights

- **Support in business hours** — From engineers experienced in Jenkins, Gitea, Terraform, and deployment; response times according to agreement (no 24/7).
- **Continuous improvement** — Pipeline and documentation evolve with your needs; subscription or per-sprint allows iterative engagement.
- **Tailored scope** — Each engagement has written scope, revision rounds, and pricing; no hidden costs.
- **SLA and response** — Reaction and resolution according to the subscribed plan or contract; no dedicated Technical Account Manager unless agreed separately.

### Highlights (caja resumen)

- **CI/CD as a Service** with pipeline operation and deployment; **support in business hours** (we do not offer 24/7).
- **Transparent pricing** — No hidden costs. Plans from **$150–250/month** (Starter: one pipeline, one repo, one environment) or **$350–550/month** (Team: multiple repos/environments, shared registry). Custom quotes for per-sprint or one-off setup.
- **Standard tooling** — Jenkins, Gitea, Maven, Docker; Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) when applicable. Pipeline as code (Jenkinsfile) and documentation in repo.
