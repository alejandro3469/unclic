**Cards (grid)**

| Capacidad | Descripción breve |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **DevOps Advisory** | Our advisory practice delivers a clear proposal to improve your pipeline strategy: scope, tooling, and deployment model. Written deliverable and recommendations. |
| **Pipeline as Code and CI/CD** | We build pipelines (Jenkinsfile, Jenkins, Gitea) that support continuous integration and delivery: build, test, package, and deploy on every commit. |

## FAQs

**Headline**  
Your DevOps-as-a-Service questions, answered.

**Subheadline**  
Learn more about DevOps-as-a-Service with us.

**Preguntas (ejemplos)**  
- **How do we achieve pipeline stability for our clients?** — We deliver a pipeline (Jenkinsfile, Jenkins, Gitea) that runs the same steps on every execution; documentation in repo and delivery verified in the agreed environment. Scope and revision rounds are defined in the proposal.  
- **How do we improve pipeline resilience for our clients?** — By operating the pipeline and keeping it versioned and documented; optional registry for rollback; support in business hours according to the agreement.  
- **How do we achieve pipeline scalability for our clients?** — From one pipeline and one environment (Starter) to multiple repos or environments (Team); per-sprint engagement for new setups or extensions. Scope and price scale with what’s agreed in writing.
