## Pestaña: Security & trust

**Headline**  
Embedded transparency and control.

**Cuerpo**  
We don’t offer 24/7 or full observability; we offer clear scope, written agreements, and delivery verified in the agreed environment.

**Métricas o bullets (solo si son reales)**  
- **Scope by default** — Every engagement has written scope (proposal or annex); what’s not in it is not included.  
- **Revision rounds defined** — Base number of rounds (e.g. 2) included; additional rounds by annex.  
- **Documentation in repo** — Jenkinsfile and guides versioned in Git; auditable and repeatable.

*Si hay certificaciones o partners (ISO, SOC2, etc.), añadir aquí; si no, dejar solo los bullets de alcance y documentación.*
